## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-15

### Added

- Added the `dat-atomic-folder` skill for creating and reviewing reproducible DAT analysis and data folders, including layout, dotenv, output, and Justfile conventions. It includes a scaffold helper for new atomic folders.
- Added the `just-sync` skill for adding documented rsync recipes to transfer selected inputs and outputs between a laptop and HPC system.
